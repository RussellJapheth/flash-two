# Field Guide 09: Polite Courtesies & Natural Reactions

_Source: SayMei Beginner Field Guide · Speaking: Starter_  
_Corresponding SRS Deck: Pack 32 (Field Guide: Polite Reactions & Social Courtesies)_

---

## 1. Apologies: 对不起 vs 不好意思

| Phrase       | Pinyin     | Severity              | When to Use                                                |
| :----------- | :--------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **对不起**   | duìbuqǐ    | Serious / High        | Deep personal fault, serious mistakes, causing damage      |
| **不好意思** | bù hǎoyìsi | Mild / Conversational | Squeezing past in a crowd, minor delay, mild inconvenience |

- **Responses to apology**: `没关系` (_méi guānxi_ — it's alright) or `没事` (_méishì_ — no problem at all).

---

## 2. Gratitude & Modesty

- **Thank you**: `谢谢` (_xièxie_) $\rightarrow$ Response: `不客气` (_bú kèqi_ — you're welcome).
- **Humble deflection**: When complimented on your Chinese:
  - `哪里哪里，还在学呢。` (_Nǎlǐ nǎlǐ, hái zài xué ne._ — Not at all, still learning.)
- **After hard work**: `辛苦了！` (_Xīnkǔ le!_ — Thanks for your hard work!)

---

## 3. Conversational Reactions

- `真的吗？` (_Zhēn de ma?_ — Really? Are you serious?)
- `太好了！` (_Tài hǎo le!_ — That's wonderful!)
- `是吗？这样啊。` (_Shì ma? Zhèyàng a._ — Is that so? I see.)
- `慢慢来，不用着急。` (_Mànmàn lái, bú yòng zháojí._ — Take your time, no rush.)
