# Field Guide 02: Ordering Food & Restaurant Adjustments

_Source: SayMei Beginner Field Guide · Speaking: Early Beginner_  
_Corresponding SRS Deck: Pack 25 (Field Guide: Ordering Food & Restaurant Adjustments)_

---

## 1. Two Safe Request Frames

| Frame        | Style           | When to Use                                  | Example                                          |
| :----------- | :-------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| **我要……**   | Direct & common | Busy counters, street stalls, casual diners  | `我要一碗牛肉面。` (Wǒ yào yì wǎn niúròu miàn.)  |
| **我想要……** | Softer & polite | Sit-down restaurants, cafes, polite requests | `我想要一杯热茶。` (Wǒ xiǎng yào yì bēi rè chá.) |

> **Cultural Note**: Place `谢谢` (_xièxie_) **after** your complete request rather than translating every English "please".

---

## 2. Four Practical Quantity Measure Words

Mandarin requires the right measure word between the number and the noun:

| Measure Chunk | Pinyin  | Typical Food/Drink                       | Example Sentence                                      |
| :------------ | :------ | :--------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| **一杯**      | yì bēi  | Cup / glass (tea, coffee, beer)          | `我想要一杯热茶。` (A cup of hot tea.)                |
| **一碗**      | yì wǎn  | Bowl (noodles, rice, soup)               | `请给我来一碗面。` (A bowl of noodles.)               |
| **一份**      | yí fèn  | Portion / order (dumplings, cold dishes) | `我们要一份煎饺。` (An order of pan-fried dumplings.) |
| **一瓶**      | yì píng | Bottle (water, beer, soda)               | `我想要一瓶矿泉水。` (A bottle of mineral water.)     |

## 3. High-Yield Drink & Food Adjustments

### Temperature & Ice

- **去冰** (_qù bīng_): No ice
- **少冰** (_shǎo bīng_): Less ice
- **常温** (_chángwēn_): Room temperature
- **热的** (_rè de_): Hot / served warm

### Sweetness

- **半糖** (_bàn táng_): Half sugar (50%)
- **微糖** (_wēi táng_): Slight sugar (30%)
- **无糖** (_wú táng_): Sugar free (0%)

### Spice & Ingredients

- **不要辣** (_bú yào là_): No spice at all
- **微辣** (_wēi là_): Mildly spicy
- **不加香菜** (_bù jiā xiāngcài_): No cilantro/coriander
- **免葱** (_miǎn cōng_): Omit scallions / spring onions

## 4. Dining Logistics & Payment

- **Dine in vs Takeout**: `在这里吃` (_zài zhèlǐ chī_ — dine in) vs `外带` (_wàidài_ — takeout)
- **Check / Bill**: `你好，我们要买单。` (_Nǐ hǎo, wǒmen yào mǎidān._) or `结账` (_jiézhàng_)
- **Packing leftovers**: `麻烦帮我打包。` (_Máfan bāng wǒ dǎbāo._)
- **Asking for napkins**: `麻烦给我们几张纸巾。` (_Máfan gěi wǒmen jǐ zhāng zhǐjīn._)
