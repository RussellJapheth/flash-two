## 2. Daily Sequence Verbs

- **起床** (_qǐchuáng_): Get out of bed
- **洗手 / 洗澡** (_xǐ shǒu / xǐzǎo_): Wash hands / take a bath or shower
- **做饭 / 吃饱了** (_zuòfàn / chī bǎo le_): Cook / full from eating
- **出门** (_chūmén_): Leave the house (remember: keys & phone!)
- **关灯 / 睡觉** (_guān dēng / shuìjiào_): Turn off lights / go to sleep

## 3. Household Chores

- `扫地` (_sǎo dì_ — sweep the floor)
- `拖地` (_tuō dì_ — mop the floor)
- `倒垃圾` (_dào lājī_ — take out the trash)
- `晾衣服` (_liàng yīfu_ — hang clothes to dry)
