# Field Guide 10: 7-Day Travel Survival & Wayfinding

_Source: SayMei Beginner Field Guide · Travel: Confident Beginner_  
_Corresponding SRS Deck: Pack 33 (Field Guide: 7-Day Travel Survival & Wayfinding)_

---

## 1. In a Taxi (出租车)

- **Addressing the driver**: `师傅，您好！` (_Shīfu, nín hǎo!_)
- **Showing address**: `请带我去这个地址。` (_Qǐng dài wǒ qù zhège dìzhǐ._)
- **Confirming duration**: `大概要多长时间？` (_Dàgài yào duō cháng shíjiān?_)
- **Arriving**: `师傅，就在前面路口停，到了。` (_Shīfu, jiù zài qiánmian lùkǒu tíng, dào le._)

---

## 2. At the Hotel (酒店)

- **Check-in**: `你好，我预订了一个房间，办理入住。` (_Bànlǐ rùzhù._)
- **Deposit & Keycard**: `押金两百块` (_deposit 200 yuan_) / `房卡` (_room card_)
- **Breakfast hours**: `明天的早餐几点开始？` (_Zǎocān jǐ diǎn kāishǐ?_)
- **Checkout**: `退房` (_tuìfáng_ — checkout)

---

## 3. Wayfinding & Navigation (问路)

- **Go straight**: `一直往直走。` (_Yìzhí wǎng zhí zǒu._)
- **Turn left**: `走到路口往左拐。` (_Wǎng zuǒ guǎi._)
- **Turn right**: `过马路然后往右拐。` (_Wǎng yòu guǎi._)
- **Transit transfers**: `换乘地铁二号线。` (_Huànchéng dìtiě èr hào xiàn._)
- **Gates**: `登机口` (_boarding gate_), `检票口` (_ticket barrier_), `出站口` (_station exit_)
