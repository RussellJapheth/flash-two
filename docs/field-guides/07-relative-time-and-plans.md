## 1. The 5-Day & 3-Week Relative Grid

```
  [前天]  <---  [昨天]  <---  [今天]  --->  [明天]  --->  [后天]
qiántiān      zuótiān       jīntiān       míngtiān      hòutiān
(2 days ago)  (yesterday)    (today)      (tomorrow)  (in 2 days)
```

| Time Scope | Past ("Up / Before")   | Present ("This")       | Future ("Down / Next") |
| :--------- | :--------------------- | :--------------------- | :--------------------- |
| **Day**    | 昨天 (_yesterday_)     | 今天 (_today_)         | 明天 (_tomorrow_)      |
| **Week**   | 上个星期 (_last week_) | 这个星期 (_this week_) | 下个星期 (_next week_) |
| **Month**  | 上个月 (_last month_)  | 这个月 (_this month_)  | 下个月 (_next month_)  |
| **Year**   | 去年 (_last year_)     | 今年 (_this year_)     | 明年 (_next year_)     |

## 2. Sentence Architecture: Where Does Time Go?

In Mandarin, point-in-time words (when an event happens) **never** go at the end of a sentence (unlike English). They occur before the verb, either before or after the subject (note: duration phrases like 三个小时 follow the verb):

$$\text{Subject} + \mathbf{\text{Time}} + \text{Verb} + \text{Object}$$
$$\text{or } \mathbf{\text{Time}} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb} + \text{Object}$$

- **Correct**: `我明天下午去图书馆。` (_Wǒ míngtiān xiàwǔ qù túshūguǎn._)
- **Correct**: `明天下午我去图书馆。` (_Míngtiān xiàwǔ wǒ qù túshūguǎn._)
- **WRONG**: <s>我去图书馆明天下午。</s>

## 3. Planning & Scheduling Phrases

- `你有空吗？` (_Nǐ yǒu kòng ma?_ — Are you free / available?)
- `真不巧，我没空。` (_Zhēn bù qiǎo, wǒ méi kòng._ — Unfortunately, I'm not free.)
- `改天再聚吧！` (_Gǎitiān zài jù ba!_ — Let's meet up another day!)
- `提前半小时到达` (_tíqián bàn xiǎoshí dàodá_ — arrive half an hour early)
- `推迟到下周` (_tuīchí dào xià zhōu_ — postponed until next week)
